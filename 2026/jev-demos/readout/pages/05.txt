Fretpaper

Fretpaper turns a guitar or bass recording into readable tablature.

Version 2.4. macOS 14 and later. 38 MB.

Drop in a WAV, AIFF, MP3 or M4A file. Fretpaper detects pitch, picks fret positions that a hand can actually reach, and writes tab you can print or export as MusicXML, MIDI or PDF. It runs on your machine. Nothing is uploaded.

Accuracy: 94% correct notes on clean single-track electric guitar, 81% on acoustic fingerstyle, 62% on distorted rhythm parts with heavy palm muting. We measured this on 120 recordings we transcribed by hand first. Distorted rhythm is our weak spot. We say so.
