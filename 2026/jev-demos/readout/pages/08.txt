Why people choose Snaplinn

Instant scanning. Point your camera at any receipt, and the total, date, and merchant are captured right away. Crumpled paper and faded printing are supported.

Smart categories. Groceries, travel, fuel, dining and more are sorted for you. You can also make your own categories whenever you want.

Works offline. No signal is needed. Your scans are processed on your device and stay there until you choose to export them.

Export anytime. Send to CSV, PDF or your spreadsheet of choice with one tap. Perfect for tax season and expense reports.

Search that finds it. Type "coffee March" and the receipt appears. No folders, no shoeboxes, no stress.

Warranty reminders. Snaplinn notices big purchases and reminds you before the return window closes.

Multi-currency. Travel with confidence. Over 140 currencies are recognized and converted automatically.

Simple, friendly pricing

Snaplinn is free to start. Scan your first receipts and explore every core feature at no cost. When you are ready for unlimited scanning, cloud backup and advanced exports, upgrade to Snaplinn Plus at a low monthly price. Cancel whenever you like.

Frequently asked

Is my data private? Yes. Scans are processed on your device. Nothing is shared with third parties.

Which devices are supported? Snaplinn runs on iPhone and iPad. More platforms are coming soon.

Do I need an account? No account is required to begin. An account is only needed if you want backup across devices.

Can I use it for business? Many users do. Exports are formatted to work with common accounting tools.
